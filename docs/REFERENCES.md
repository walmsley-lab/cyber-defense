# References and research provenance

## Frameworks
- [MITRE ATT&CK Enterprise](https://attack.mitre.org/matrices/enterprise/) and [Cloud](https://attack.mitre.org/matrices/enterprise/cloud/) — tactics and techniques.
- [MITRE CWE](https://cwe.mitre.org/) — weakness classes.
- [MITRE CAPEC](https://capec.mitre.org/) — attack patterns.
- [CVE](https://www.cve.org/) and [NIST NVD](https://nvd.nist.gov/) — vulnerability references.
